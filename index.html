No node knows the global shape. No target coordinates exist. Each node knows only nearby neighbors, preferred local bond lengths, collision avoidance, damping, and—where relevant—a tiny local memory. The six panels alter those local conditions. If the larger form persists or returns, that behavior is produced by the rules themselves.

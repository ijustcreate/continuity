CONTINUITY

Seven experiments. One self-organizing organism. This version is built around an explicit experimental sequence: establish a stable body, intervene on demand, then watch recovery or failure. The organism is a soft ring maintained by local neighbor bonds, short-range repulsion, energy, and local bond adaptation. No particle stores a global silhouette or target coordinate.

A panel is allowed to fail. The intervention is the experiment; the caption is only the hypothesis.
